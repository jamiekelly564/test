# Drawing sources and model adaptation licences

The source previews are resized/conversion copies of the exact public source files identified below. No client floor plans or Marketfield materials are part of this collection. The models are manually simplified selected-floor adaptations, not measured surveys. Original source hashes are recorded in sources.json.

ShareAlike source adaptations remain under the corresponding CC BY-SA licence (3.0 or 4.0). Models adapted from public-domain plans are offered under CC0 1.0. These licences apply to the respective drawing/model data, not a blanket licence for unrelated PropertyChecked application code. Attribution must accompany reuse.
